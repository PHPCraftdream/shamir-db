# Brief — SEED-2 follow-up: make the maintenance open real

You reported SEED-2 as partial: `MaintenanceOpen::open` refuses because `ShamirDb::init` reaches `RepoInstance::repo_wal` (starts the WAL fsync owner) and later drainer/flush access starts another write owner, neither with a join handle. DECISION (binding, from the owner's design review): **those two repository-internal tasks are ACCEPTED in the maintenance open.** They perform no work without a writer; once cold recovery has completed there is no in-flight WAL entry and no client can exist (the exclusive maintenance lock is held and no listener is ever bound). The contract "no write-producing jobs" means: NO listeners, NO replication/subscription supervisors, NO scheduler, NO audit appender/meta-snapshot task, NO user connections, NO CREATE/DROP of anything. It does NOT forbid the WAL fsync owner, the lazily created drainer or the changefeed writer.
