# Brief — SEED-2 follow-up 2: do not refuse sources with sorted / index2 indexes

Review of your second pass: the maintenance open, lock, pid write, export twice test and decoder bounds are accepted. ONE functional defect remains: `export_open` refuses every table that has a sorted index or a non-empty index2 registry ("registry materialization cap is zero"). That makes the seed useless for exactly the repositories that matter (vector / full-text / sorted indexes are first-class features), and the stated reason does not hold:

- the tables are ALREADY open in this process, so those registries are already resident in memory; cloning the descriptors once is at most a constant factor over what the maintenance open already holds, it is not a repository-sized allocation;
- the export policy is `index_policy = "EXCLUDED"`: the seed never carries index data, the descriptors are recorded only so the installer knows what was left out.
